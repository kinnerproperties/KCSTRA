How the data is produced

  1. Find the listings. Airbnb search run through a third-party scraping service on its own servers, all 40 KCMO zip codes plus neighbourhood, price-band and date slices. No Airbnb account is used. Every pin is tested against the City of KCMO boundary polygon; listings outside are dropped. Listings that only appear for 30-night-or-longer searches are mid-term rentals, which need no permit, and are excluded.
  2. Read the number. The Registration Details text the host typed. It is not an official Airbnb field.
  3. Registry. Each number is compared to the city's live short-term rental registry (currently issued permits only). Prefix variants are matched.
  4. CompassKC. Every cited number is looked up for status (Issued, Expired, Denied, not found), type (Resident, Non-Resident, Major Event) and the parcel it belongs to.
  5. Parcel rule. A parcel holding a currently issued permit under a newer number counts as permitted even when the listing shows an old number. Validated on a known-permitted portfolio.
  6. Borrowed-permit check. A permitted verdict whose permit parcel is more than 1 km from the listing pin becomes "Cites a permit for a different property". Pins are fuzzed by at most about 150 m.
  7. Distance. Nearest currently issued permit to each pin. Over 200 m means no permitted STR is anywhere near.
  8. Owner of record. Parcel owner and mailing address from the Parcel Viewer layer, by city parcel ID.
  9. Units and density. Sec. 56-803 registers each dwelling unit separately, allows no more than one STR per eight units in a building of three or more (registration stops at 12.5%), and keeps non-resident STRs in one- and two-unit structures 1,000 feet apart. A number shown on two or more entire-home listings that are different units, or a building over the cap per the assessor's unit count and the live registry, is flagged.
  10. Hotels. Hotel brands and hotel-room resellers claiming Exempt are separated out and not counted.

Each audit run produces a dated snapshot. This page is a viewer over those snapshots; pick one at the top to compare runs.

Rules

  • Never present "old number on a permitted parcel" as a violation.
  • Mid-term (30+ night) listings need no permit.
  • Hotels, motels and bed-and-breakfasts sit outside the program and are separated out. A dwelling that says "Exempt" is making a claim the ordinance does not provide for, so it counts as a contradicted number.
  • Every row links to its public source; nothing here is private data.
